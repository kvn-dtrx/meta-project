# Projects

## Synopsis

This directory encompasses the entirety of our undertakings — the term to be understood in its most expansive sense.

## Directory Organisation

The projects are arranged according to their respective stages of advancement:

- `archive`: Are no longer in active development, retained solely for reference.
- `permanent`: Are dependendent on a stable absolute path.
- `review`: Require editorial attention, albeit not of pressing urgency, to be undertaken only when ample leisure permits.
- `running`: Are presently in preparation and likely to be developed over the coming days.
- `staged`: May be regarded as concluded, at least for the present time.
